# Evolution simulator

This is my first major programming project. Written entirely in Python, it utilises many programming concepts such as database management, OOP, Perlin Noise, decision trees, and the A* pathfinding algorithm to procedurally generate and simulate environments of very simple flora and fauna. The fauna in these environments possess traits, determining their speed, sight, and ability to control their temperature - the difference in these traits are reflected by their colour. The better their traits are, the faster their energy will deplete, requiring them to eat and drink more often in order to survive. They can also die from being too cold or too hot. These fauna will interact with the environment, flora, and with each other. They will reproduce, creating offspring with traits similar to their parents. Overtime, this leads to the average traits of the population evolving in accordance to the environment, showcasing how even with simple conditions, evolution still naturally occurs. The program harnesses the Pygame library to visually display these simulations, in addition to using other libraries handling backend functions such as NumPy and sqlite3.
